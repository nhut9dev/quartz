**Enrollment** links a learner to a course, cohort, class, program, or subscription.

It controls access, progress tracking, deadlines, grades, certificates, billing eligibility, and instructor visibility.

Enrollment is often more than a join table because it has status, start date, completion date, payment state, transfer history, and cancellation rules.
