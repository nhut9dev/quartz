A **queue** is a first-in, first-out data structure. The oldest item added is the first item removed.

```txt
enqueue A
enqueue B
dequeue -> A
```

Queues are useful for scheduling, background jobs, event processing, buffering, and [[breadth-first-search|breadth-first search]].

Typical operations are `enqueue`, `dequeue`, and `peek`.
