**White space** is empty space that separates, groups, and gives content room to be understood.

White space does not have to be white. It can be any unoccupied area around text, controls, images, or sections.

Use it to reduce cognitive load, create rhythm, and make important content easier to find. Too little space makes a screen feel crowded; too much can weaken relationships and reduce information density.

White space is closely related to [[spacing|spacing]] and [[layout-and-composition|layout and composition]].
