A **lease agreement** is the contract that defines rental terms between landlord and tenant.

It usually covers parties, property, dates, rent, deposit, fees, responsibilities, renewal terms, termination rules, and signatures.

Systems should preserve signed versions and avoid silently mutating contractual terms after agreement. Amendments and renewals should be modeled as new records or explicit changes.
