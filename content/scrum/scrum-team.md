A **Scrum Team** is a small, cross-functional team accountable for creating a valuable [[increment|Increment]] each [[sprint|Sprint]].

It has three accountabilities: [[product-owner|Product Owner]], [[scrum-master|Scrum Master]], and [[developers|Developers]].

Scrum Teams are self-managing. They decide who does what, when, and how, within the product direction and organizational constraints around them.

The team should contain the skills needed to turn selected backlog work into a done increment without depending on a separate handoff chain.
