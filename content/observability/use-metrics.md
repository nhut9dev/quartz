**USE metrics** are utilization, saturation, and errors.

They fit resources such as CPU, memory, disks, queues, network links, and connection pools. Utilization asks how busy the resource is. Saturation asks how much work is waiting. Errors show failed operations.

USE complements [[red-metrics|RED metrics]] by explaining whether user-visible symptoms come from resource pressure.
