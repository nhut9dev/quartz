**RAG** stands for retrieval-augmented generation.

The application retrieves relevant documents or data, then provides them to the [[llm|model]] as [[context-window|context]] for an answer.

RAG helps ground answers in external knowledge, but it requires careful retrieval, citation, chunking, and handling of missing information.
