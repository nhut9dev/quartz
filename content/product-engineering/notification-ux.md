**Notification UX** decides what deserves attention, where it appears, and how users control it.

Notifications should be timely, actionable, and proportional to importance. Over-notifying makes users ignore the channel; under-notifying hides important events.

Design choices include batching, priority, quiet hours, unsubscribe controls, read state, and fallback channels. Related system design: [[../system-design/notification-system|Notification system]].
