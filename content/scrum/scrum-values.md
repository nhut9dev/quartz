The **Scrum values** are commitment, focus, openness, respect, and courage.

These values are not slogans. They describe the behavior Scrum needs in order to work: focus on the [[sprint-goal|Sprint Goal]], openness about progress and problems, respect across disciplines, courage to surface hard truths, and commitment to shared goals.

When the values are weak, Scrum events can still happen, but they become mechanical. The team may meet often without actually improving the product or the way it works.

Scrum depends on trust because it is designed to reveal reality quickly.
