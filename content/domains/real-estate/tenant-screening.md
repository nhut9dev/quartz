**Tenant screening** evaluates rental applicants before approving a lease.

It can include identity verification, income documents, employment checks, rental history, references, background checks, credit checks, and landlord review.

Because screening uses sensitive data and affects housing decisions, access control, consent, auditability, and retention rules matter.
