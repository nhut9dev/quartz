tags:

---

**Sprint Planning** starts the [[sprint|Sprint]]. The team decides why the Sprint is valuable, what work can be done, and how that work will be approached.

The main outputs are a [[sprint-goal|Sprint Goal]] and a [[sprint-backlog|Sprint Backlog]].

```txt
why -> Sprint Goal
what -> selected backlog items
how -> initial delivery plan
```

Good planning is not about predicting every task perfectly. It creates enough shared understanding for the team to start and adapt.
