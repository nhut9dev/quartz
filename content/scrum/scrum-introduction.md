**Scrum** is a lightweight framework for teams building complex products. It organizes work into [[sprint|Sprints]], creates regular feedback points, and makes progress visible through artifacts like the [[product-backlog|Product Backlog]], [[sprint-backlog|Sprint Backlog]], and [[increment|Increment]].

Scrum does not prescribe every engineering practice. It defines accountabilities, events, artifacts, and commitments so a team can inspect reality and adapt.

The core idea is [[empiricism|empiricism]]: make decisions from observed evidence rather than long upfront prediction.

Scrum works best when teams use it to expose problems, not to hide uncertainty behind ceremonies.
