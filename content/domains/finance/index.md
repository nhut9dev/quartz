**Finance domain knowledge** covers how money is represented, moved, recorded, checked, and controlled.

This folder is intentionally split into two tracks:

- [[business/|Finance business knowledge]] explains financial concepts, entities, workflows, and vocabulary.
- [[engineering/|Finance engineering knowledge]] explains implementation concerns such as precision, idempotency, auditability, consistency, and failure handling.

Use the business notes to understand what the domain means. Use the engineering notes to connect those rules to [[../../database/transaction|transactions]], [[../../api-design/idempotency-key|idempotency keys]], [[../../security/|security]], [[../../observability/|observability]], and [[../../system-design/|system design]].
