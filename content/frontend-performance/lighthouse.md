**Lighthouse** audits a page and reports scores, diagnostics, and opportunities.

It is useful for catching common performance, accessibility, SEO, and best-practice issues.

Treat Lighthouse as a lab signal. It does not replace [[core-web-vitals|real user monitoring]], production metrics, or task-specific [[profiling]].
