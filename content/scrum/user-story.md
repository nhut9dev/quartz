A **user story** is a short backlog item written from a user's perspective.

```txt
As a team admin,
I want to invite members by email,
so that I can onboard my team.
```

User stories are a format, not a Scrum requirement. Their value is in focusing conversation on user need and outcome.

A good story should be small enough to discuss, refine, and complete within a [[sprint|Sprint]], with clear [[acceptance-criteria|acceptance criteria]].
