**Managing underperformance** is the work of helping an engineer who is consistently falling short, and deciding what to do if support does not close the gap. It is uncomfortable but unavoidable.

Start by diagnosing, not blaming. Is it a skills gap, unclear expectations, a bad role fit, or something happening outside work? Each cause points to a different response.

Clear, direct, early feedback is the kindest move. Letting problems drift hurts the person, their team, and [[team-health|team health]], and makes any later decision feel like a surprise.

When informal feedback is not enough, a written support plan sets specific, time-bound goals so success or failure is unambiguous for everyone.

If the gap persists, parting ways may be the honest outcome. Handling it with respect protects [[psychological-safety|psychological safety]]; the failure mode is treating this work as a substitute for ongoing [[../career/giving-feedback|Giving feedback]].
