An **Increment** is a usable piece of product value created during a [[sprint|Sprint]].

It must meet the [[definition-of-done|Definition of Done]]. If it does not meet that standard, it is unfinished work, not a done increment.

There can be more than one increment in a Sprint. Scrum does not require waiting until the end of the Sprint to integrate or release value.

The Increment is what makes inspection real. Stakeholders can respond to working product instead of only plans, documents, or progress reports.
