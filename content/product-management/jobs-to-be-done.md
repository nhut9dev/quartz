title: Jobs to be done

**Jobs to be done** (JTBD) is a framework that views products through the lens of the progress users are trying to make. People don't buy products; they "hire" them to do a job in their lives.

The classic example: customers don't want a quarter-inch drill, they want a quarter-inch hole, and really they want to hang a shelf. Focusing on the job, not the product, reveals the true competition and the real need.

A job has functional, emotional, and social dimensions. Understanding all three explains why users choose, switch, or abandon a product, often for reasons a feature list misses.

JTBD reframes who your competitors are: anything a user might "hire" instead, including doing nothing. This widens your view of the market and the opportunity.

JTBD thinking sharpens [[user-research|user research]] and [[product-discovery|product discovery]], and helps write [[../scrum/user-story|user stories]] focused on outcomes rather than features.
