The **Product Backlog** is an ordered list of work needed to improve the product.

It may contain features, fixes, experiments, technical work, research, and improvements. The [[product-owner|Product Owner]] is accountable for making it visible, understood, and ordered.

Backlog items near the top should be clearer and smaller than items further down, because they are closer to being selected for a [[sprint|Sprint]].

The Product Backlog is not a storage bucket for every idea forever. It should reflect current product strategy and learning.
