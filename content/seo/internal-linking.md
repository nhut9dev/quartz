**Internal linking** is connecting pages within the same site so users and crawlers can discover related content.

### How it works

Internal links pass discovery, context, and importance signals. A page linked from navigation, an index page, and relevant articles is easier for crawlers to find and easier for users to understand.

Anchor text matters because it tells the reader and search engine what the target page is about. Links should be descriptive and contextual, not generic. In this garden, [[../templates/note|note templates]] explicitly encourage wikilinks to keep related ideas connected.

### When to use

Use internal links when one page depends on another concept, when building topic clusters, and when helping users move from a broad overview to a deeper note. It is central to [[content-seo|content SEO]].

### Trade-offs

More links improve discovery, but too many links dilute attention. Link where the relationship is useful, not every time a word appears.

### Pitfalls

Avoid orphan pages, buried important pages, repeated generic anchors, and navigation that exposes hundreds of links without prioritization.
