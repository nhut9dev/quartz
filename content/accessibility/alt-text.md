title: Alt text
description: Text alternative for images that communicates their purpose or content.
tags:

**Alt text** describes an [[image-element|image]] for people who cannot see it or when the image fails to load.

Good alt text depends on context. A product image, chart, icon, and decorative background need different treatment.

Decorative images should usually use empty alt text: `alt=""`.

Do not start with "image of" unless that distinction matters. [[screen-reader|Screen readers]] already announce that it is an image.
