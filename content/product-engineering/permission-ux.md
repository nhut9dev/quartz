**Permission UX** explains why a user can or cannot do something.

Good permission flows ask only when needed, explain the value before requesting sensitive access, and show a useful blocked state when access is denied.

For team products, permission errors should distinguish missing login, missing role, disabled feature, and unavailable resource. See [[../auth/rbac|RBAC]] and [[../security/principle-of-least-privilege|least privilege]].
