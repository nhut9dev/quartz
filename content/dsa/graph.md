A **graph** is a data structure made of nodes connected by edges.

Graphs model relationships: users and friendships, cities and roads, web pages and links, packages and dependencies, or services and network calls.

Graphs can be directed or undirected, weighted or unweighted, cyclic or acyclic. Common traversal algorithms include [[breadth-first-search|breadth-first search]] and [[depth-first-search|depth-first search]].
