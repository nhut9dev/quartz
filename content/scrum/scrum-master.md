The **Scrum Master** is accountable for the [[scrum-team|Scrum Team]]'s effective use of Scrum.

They coach the team, help remove impediments, improve collaboration, protect useful Scrum events, and help the organization understand how Scrum should work.

The Scrum Master is not a project manager assigning tasks. Scrum is built around a self-managing team, so the role serves effectiveness rather than controlling people.

A good Scrum Master makes problems visible and helps the team improve its system of work.
