A **product catalog** is the source of product data shown to customers: names, descriptions, images, categories, prices, variants, availability, and attributes.

Catalog modeling must separate a product family from its sellable variants. For example, a shoe product can have variants by size and color, each with its own SKU and inventory.

The hard parts are search relevance, faceted filtering, merchandising, SEO pages, localized content, and keeping cached catalog pages consistent with price and inventory changes.
