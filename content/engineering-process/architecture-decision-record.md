An **architecture decision record** (ADR) captures one significant architectural decision, its context, and its consequences.

Each ADR is short and immutable: it records the decision as made, including the forces and alternatives, rather than being edited later. Superseding decisions get new ADRs that link back.

ADRs are usually kept in the repository alongside the code, so the reasoning travels with the system it shaped.

The value shows up months later, when someone asks "why is it built this way?" and finds the answer instead of guessing.

An ADR records the outcome of an [[rfc|RFC]] or design discussion, and strengthens the team's [[documentation-culture|documentation culture]].
