---
title: Screen reader
description: Assistive technology that reads interface content and semantics aloud or through braille.
tags:
  - accessibility
---

A **screen reader** helps users navigate and understand software without relying on visual output.

Screen readers depend on [[semantic-html|semantic HTML]], [[accessible-name|accessible names]], roles, states, focus order, and [[aria-live|live announcements]].

Good visual design does not guarantee screen reader usability. Test flows with [[keyboard-navigation|keyboard navigation]] and at least one real screen reader when possible.
