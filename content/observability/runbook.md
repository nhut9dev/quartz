A **runbook** documents how to investigate and respond to a known operational issue.

It usually includes symptoms, dashboards, useful queries, ownership, rollback steps, and escalation paths.

Runbooks turn [[alerting|alerts]] into action by giving responders a clear first path through the problem.

They should be updated after incidents, deploy changes, and repeated manual fixes.
