A **payroll run** calculates and finalizes employee pay for a pay period.

It combines salary, hourly time, overtime, bonuses, deductions, taxes, benefits, reimbursements, leave, and corrections.

Payroll needs strong controls because mistakes directly affect employees and accounting. A run should be reviewable, auditable, and reversible through adjustments rather than silent mutation.
