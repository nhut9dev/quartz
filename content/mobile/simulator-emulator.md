title: Simulator and emulator
description: Desktop tools for running mobile apps without a physical device.
tags:

A **simulator** or **emulator** runs a mobile environment on a development machine. It lets developers test apps without installing them on a physical device every time.

iOS commonly uses the iOS Simulator. Android commonly uses the Android Emulator.

These tools are useful for fast development, screenshots, debugging, and testing multiple screen sizes.

They are not perfect replacements for real devices. Performance, camera, sensors, push notifications, and device-specific behavior can differ.
