**Spacing** is the controlled distance between elements in a layout.

Spacing shows what belongs together and what is separate. Tight spacing implies relation; larger spacing creates groups, sections, and pauses. It works with [[white-space|white space]], [[alignment|alignment]], and [[visual-hierarchy|visual hierarchy]].

A spacing scale keeps layouts consistent. Values such as 4, 8, 12, 16, 24, and 32 pixels are easier to reason about than one-off distances chosen by eye.

In product UI, inconsistent spacing often feels more broken than a mediocre color choice because it weakens scanning and makes repeated patterns hard to recognize.
