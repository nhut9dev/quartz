**Velocity** is the amount of estimated work a team completes over Sprints, often measured in [[story-point|story points]].

It is useful for rough forecasting when the team, work type, and estimation style are stable.

Velocity is not productivity by itself. Increasing the number can mean better flow, weaker quality standards, inflated estimates, or pressure on the team.

Use velocity as a planning signal for the team, not as a performance metric to compare people or teams.
