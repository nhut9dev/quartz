---
title: SSO
description: Single sign-on, where one identity provider authenticates users across applications.
tags:
  - auth
---

**SSO** lets users sign in once through an identity provider and access multiple applications.

It improves user experience and centralizes account management.

SSO often uses standards such as SAML or [[openid-connect|OpenID Connect]].

Applications still need their own authorization rules, such as [[rbac|role-based access control]], after identity is established.
