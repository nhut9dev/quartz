A **north-star metric** is the single metric that best captures the core value a product delivers to its users. It gives the whole team one number to rally around and align decisions toward.

A strong north-star metric reflects customer value, not just revenue. Spotify tracks time spent listening; Airbnb tracks nights booked. When the metric goes up, users are genuinely getting more value.

It sits above a small set of input metrics that teams can directly influence, forming a tree from daily work up to long-term value. Moving the inputs should move the star.

Beware optimizing a metric that drifts from real value, a classic vanity-metric trap. Pair the north star with guardrail [[product-metrics|metrics]] so growth doesn't come at the cost of quality.

The north-star metric anchors the [[product-roadmap|roadmap]] and complements broader frameworks like [[aarrr-metrics|AARRR metrics]]. Instrument it with reliable [[../product-engineering/analytics-event-tracking|analytics event tracking]].
