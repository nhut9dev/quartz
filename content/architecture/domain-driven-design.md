---
title: Domain-driven design
description: Modeling software around business concepts and language.
tags:
  - architecture
---

**Domain-driven design** models software around the business domain instead of starting from database tables or technical layers.

The team uses a shared vocabulary, called ubiquitous language, so code names match the language used by domain experts.

DDD is most useful when business rules are complex and the cost of misunderstanding them is high.

It often leads to clearer [[../web/service-layer|service boundaries]], stronger module ownership, and fewer generic data models that hide important rules.
