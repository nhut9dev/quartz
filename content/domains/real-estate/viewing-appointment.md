A **viewing appointment** is a scheduled visit for a buyer, renter, tenant, owner, or agent to inspect a property.

The workflow includes availability, booking, confirmation, rescheduling, cancellation, reminders, access instructions, attendance, and follow-up.

Scheduling gets difficult when multiple agents, occupied properties, lockboxes, time zones, and last-minute cancellations are involved.
