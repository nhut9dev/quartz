**AARRR metrics**, nicknamed "pirate metrics," break the user lifecycle into five stages: Acquisition, Activation, Retention, Referral, and Revenue. The framework gives growth teams a shared map of where users succeed or drop off.

*Acquisition* is how users find you; *Activation* is their first real experience of value. Many products lose more users to weak activation than to weak acquisition.

*Retention* measures whether users come back, and it's usually the truest signal of value. *Referral* captures users bringing in others, and *Revenue* captures monetization.

Treat AARRR as a funnel: measure conversion between stages to find the biggest leak, then focus effort there. Fixing the worst stage often beats optimizing a healthy one.

AARRR complements the [[north-star-metric|north-star metric]] and informs the [[product-roadmap|roadmap]]. Improve each stage through [[ab-testing|A/B testing]] and [[user-research|user research]], measured via [[../product-engineering/analytics-event-tracking|analytics event tracking]].
