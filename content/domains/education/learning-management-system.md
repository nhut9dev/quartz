A **learning management system** manages courses, learners, instructors, classes, progress, assessments, certificates, and reporting.

LMS workflows have multiple roles with different views of the same data: learner, instructor, parent, manager, school administrator, and platform operator.

Engineering concerns include permission boundaries, content delivery performance, progress event capture, gradebook consistency, and reporting over historical cohorts.
