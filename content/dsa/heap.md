A **heap** is a tree-based structure where the highest or lowest priority item is kept at the top.

In a min-heap, the smallest item is at the root. In a max-heap, the largest item is at the root.

Heaps are commonly implemented with arrays. They are useful for [[priority-queue|priority queues]], scheduling, top-k problems, and algorithms that repeatedly need the next best item.
