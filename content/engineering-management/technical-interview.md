title: Technical interview

A **technical interview** assesses how a candidate solves problems, not whether they have memorized a specific trick. The aim is real signal about how they would work on the job.

The strongest formats resemble the actual work: debugging real code, extending a small system, or talking through a design. Trivia and obscure puzzles mostly measure luck and recall.

Fairness comes from structure. Use a consistent rubric, give every candidate the same prompt and hints, and score against defined criteria rather than gut feel.

Interviewing is a skill that needs calibration. Interviewers should compare notes on past loops so a "yes" means the same thing across the team.

Each session feeds a scorecard in the wider [[hiring-process|hiring process]]. Calibrating against the [[career-ladder|career ladder]] ensures the bar matches the level being hired.
