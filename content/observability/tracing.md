---
title: Tracing
description: Tracking one request or workflow across services and components.
tags:
  - observability
---

**Tracing** follows a request as it moves through services, databases, queues, and external APIs.

A trace is made of spans, where each span represents one unit of work.

Tracing is useful for debugging latency, dependency failures, and distributed workflows that [[logging|logs]] alone cannot explain.
