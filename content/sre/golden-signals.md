The **golden signals** are latency, traffic, errors, and saturation.

They give a compact view of whether a service is healthy from the user's perspective and whether the system is running out of resources.

They are a starting point, not a complete dashboard. Pair them with product-specific signals, dependency health, and [[../observability/slo-sli|SLO and SLI]] definitions.
