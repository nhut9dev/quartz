---
title: Psychological safety
description: A team climate where people can speak up and fail without fear.
tags:
  - engineering-management
  - leadership
---

**Psychological safety** is a shared belief that the team is safe for interpersonal risk: asking questions, admitting mistakes, and disagreeing without fear of punishment or ridicule.

It is not about being nice or avoiding conflict. Safe teams argue hard about ideas precisely because no one fears being attacked for raising one.

Leaders build it through their own behavior: admitting their own mistakes, responding to bad news with curiosity instead of blame, and treating questions as valuable.

Without it, problems stay hidden until they are expensive. Engineers hide bugs, skip hard questions, and stop flagging risks, which quietly erodes [[team-health|team health]].

Safety is reinforced in [[one-on-one|one-on-ones]], blameless incident reviews, and how [[../career/giving-feedback|feedback]] is given. It underpins honest [[performance-review|performance reviews]].
