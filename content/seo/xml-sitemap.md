**XML sitemap** is a machine-readable list of important URLs that search engines should discover.

A sitemap helps crawlers find canonical pages, especially on large sites, new sites, or pages that are not yet well linked internally. It should include indexable URLs that return `200` and should generally agree with [[canonical-url|canonical URLs]].

Sitemaps do not guarantee indexing or ranking. They are a discovery aid, not a replacement for good [[internal-linking|internal linking]].

Avoid putting redirected, blocked, duplicate, `noindex`, or broken URLs in the sitemap. That makes search engines waste crawl budget and can hide real quality issues.
