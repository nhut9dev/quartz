**Healthcare privacy** protects sensitive patient information from inappropriate access, disclosure, and retention.

Privacy is not only encryption. It includes who can access which data, for what purpose, under what relationship, and with what audit trail. Operational needs such as emergency access, billing, support, and clinical care may require different controls.

Engineering concerns include [[../../auth/rbac|RBAC]], [[../../auth/abac|ABAC]], [[../../security/encryption-at-rest|encryption at rest]], audit logging, data minimization, consent, retention, and secure sharing with external systems.
