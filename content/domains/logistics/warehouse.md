A **warehouse** is a physical location that receives, stores, picks, packs, and ships goods.

Warehouse workflows revolve around bins, stock movements, receiving, putaway, picking, packing, cycle counts, and exceptions.

Software must treat inventory as a changing physical fact, not only a database number. Scans, manual adjustments, damaged goods, and delayed synchronization can all create mismatch.
