The **Daily Scrum** is a short daily event for [[developers|Developers]] to inspect progress toward the [[sprint-goal|Sprint Goal]] and adapt the plan for the next day.

It is not a status meeting for managers. The audience is the Developers, and the focus is coordination.

The familiar three questions can help, but they are not required. What matters is whether the team leaves with a clearer plan.

Problems discovered in Daily Scrum can be discussed immediately after with the people needed, instead of turning the event into a long meeting.
