title: Kanban

**Kanban** is a flow-based method for managing work by visualizing it on a board and limiting how much is in progress at once.

Work moves through columns from "to do" to "done," and each stage has a **WIP limit** that caps how many items can sit there. When a column is full, no new work enters until something leaves.

This makes it a pull system: capacity pulls work forward instead of work being pushed in. Limiting WIP exposes bottlenecks and shortens the time any single item takes to finish.

Unlike timeboxed sprints, Kanban has no fixed iteration; work flows continuously and is reprioritized as the queue empties.

Kanban optimizes for cycle time and flow, complementing the forecasting that [[estimation|estimation]] and [[../scrum/velocity|velocity]] provide, and pairs naturally with [[dora-metrics|DORA metrics]].
