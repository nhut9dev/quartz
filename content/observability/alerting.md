---
title: Alerting
description: Notifying people or systems when action is needed.
tags:
  - observability
---

**Alerting** turns telemetry, such as [[metrics|metrics]], into notifications when a system likely needs attention.

Good alerts are actionable, urgent, and tied to user impact, often driven by [[slo-sli|SLO]] burn rather than raw thresholds.

Avoid alerts for every noisy symptom. Alert fatigue makes real incidents easier to miss.
