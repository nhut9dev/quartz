---
title: Embedding
description: A numeric representation of text, images, or other data for similarity search.
tags:
  - ai
  - llm
---

An **embedding** represents data as a [[vector-database|vector]] of numbers.

Similar items should have vectors that are close together in embedding space.

Embeddings are commonly used for semantic search, clustering, recommendations, deduplication, and [[rag|retrieval-augmented generation]].
