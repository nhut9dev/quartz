**Backlog refinement** is ongoing work to make [[product-backlog|Product Backlog]] items clearer, smaller, better ordered, and better understood.

Refinement can include splitting work, clarifying value, adding [[acceptance-criteria|acceptance criteria]], estimating, removing stale items, and identifying dependencies.

Scrum does not define refinement as an official event, but most teams need it to keep [[sprint-planning|Sprint Planning]] effective.

Refinement should improve readiness without pretending to fully design all future work upfront.
