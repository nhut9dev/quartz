An **approval workflow** controls when a business action can proceed.

Approvals are common for purchases, refunds, discounts, access changes, vendor onboarding, and financial adjustments. The workflow usually depends on amount, department, role, risk, policy, and escalation rules.

Good approval systems preserve who requested the action, who approved it, when each decision happened, and whether the underlying request changed after approval. They connect strongly to [[../../auth/rbac|RBAC]], [[../../auth/abac|ABAC]], notifications, and audit trails.
