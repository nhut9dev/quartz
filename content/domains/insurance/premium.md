A **premium** is the amount a customer pays for insurance coverage over a policy period.

Premium calculation can depend on coverage, limits, deductibles, risk factors, discounts, taxes, fees, billing frequency, and policy changes.

Premium systems must handle prorations, cancellations, renewals, refunds, failed payments, and reconciliation with accounting records.
