**Estimation** is the act of sizing the effort and uncertainty of work before the team commits to it.

Estimates are forecasts under uncertainty, not promises; their accuracy drops sharply the larger and less understood the work is. Breaking work down shrinks the unknowns and tightens the range.

Relative sizing often beats absolute time, since people compare two tasks more reliably than they predict raw hours. That is why teams reach for points instead of dates.

The value of estimating is mostly in the conversation it forces, surfacing hidden assumptions and missing requirements before they cost real time.

Estimation feeds planning through [[../scrum/story-point|story points]] and [[../scrum/velocity|velocity]], and is distorted when [[technical-debt|technical debt]] inflates the cost of routine changes.
