A **customer account** is the organization or person a business sells to, supports, and tracks over time.

Account records aggregate contacts, opportunities, contracts, invoices, usage, support tickets, renewals, and health signals.

The hard part is identity and ownership: one real company can have subsidiaries, regions, duplicate records, multiple buyers, and different account teams.
