The **Sprint Backlog** is the [[sprint-goal|Sprint Goal]], the selected [[product-backlog|Product Backlog]] items for the Sprint, and the plan for delivering them.

It belongs to the [[developers|Developers]]. They update it as they learn more during the [[sprint|Sprint]].

The Sprint Backlog is more than a task list. It connects day-to-day work to the Sprint Goal.

If the plan becomes unrealistic, the team should adapt the Sprint Backlog while protecting the Sprint Goal where possible.
