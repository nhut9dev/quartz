The **IC vs management track** describes two parallel ways to grow once an engineer reaches a senior level. One deepens technical impact; the other shifts to leading people.

The **individual contributor (IC) track** rewards technical depth, system ownership, and influence across teams. Senior ICs like staff and principal engineers drive architecture and standards without managing reports.

The **management track** rewards growing people, setting direction, and multiplying a team's output. Managers own hiring, [[one-on-one|one-on-ones]], and [[performance-review|performance reviews]].

The tracks are usually designed to reach equal seniority and pay, so neither is a "promotion" over the other. Switching between them is common and expected.

A [[tech-lead|tech lead]] role is a useful way to try leadership without leaving the IC track. The [[career-ladder|career ladder]] defines what each level expects on both sides.
