A **one-on-one** is a recurring private meeting between a manager and a report, owned by the report. It is the report's time to raise whatever matters to them, not a status update.

Common agenda items are blockers, career growth, feedback in both directions, and how the person is actually feeling. Status belongs in async updates or standups instead.

Consistency matters more than length. A reliable weekly or biweekly 1:1 builds the trust that makes hard conversations possible later.

The manager mostly listens and asks questions. This is a primary channel for [[../career/mentoring|Mentoring]] and [[../career/giving-feedback|Giving feedback]].

Skipping 1:1s repeatedly is an early warning sign for [[team-health|team health]] and erodes [[psychological-safety|psychological safety]].
