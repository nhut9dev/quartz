---
title: Color contrast
description: The difference in brightness between foreground and background colors.
tags:
  - accessibility
  - design
---

**Color contrast** is the difference between foreground and background colors. Text needs enough contrast to stay readable for users with low vision, color blindness, or poor display conditions.

Contrast is especially important for small text, disabled states, [[anchor-and-link|links]], [[html-form-validation|form errors]], and text over images.

Color should not be the only way to communicate meaning. Pair color with text, icons, borders, or layout changes so the information remains available without relying on color perception alone.
