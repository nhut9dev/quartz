title: Sprint Goal
description: The single objective that gives a Sprint coherence and focus.

The **Sprint Goal** is the single objective for a [[sprint|Sprint]]. It explains why the selected work matters together.

```txt
Improve new-user activation by letting users complete onboarding without support.
```

The Sprint Goal gives the team flexibility. If one backlog item turns out to be wrong or too large, the team can adapt the plan while still pursuing the same goal.

A Sprint without a real goal often becomes a bundle of unrelated tickets.
