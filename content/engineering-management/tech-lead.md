title: Tech lead

A **tech lead** owns the technical direction of a team's work while remaining hands-on in the code. The role blends architecture, light coordination, and mentoring rather than formal people management.

The tech lead sets technical standards, breaks down ambiguous work, and unblocks the team. They make the call when there is no obvious right answer, and they explain the tradeoffs so the team can disagree informed.

Unlike a manager, a tech lead usually has no reports and does not own hiring or [[performance-review|performance reviews]]. Their influence comes from credibility and clarity, not authority.

The role is a common bridge on the [[ic-vs-management-track|individual contributor track]] and overlaps with the [[../scrum/developers|Developers]] accountability in Scrum. Tech leads grow the team through [[../career/mentoring|Mentoring]] and pairing.

Good tech leads protect [[team-health|team health]] by keeping scope realistic and saying no when needed.
