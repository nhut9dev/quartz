title: Push notification
description: A message delivered to a mobile device by the platform notification service.

---

A **push notification** is a message delivered to a mobile device through platform notification services, even when the app is not open.

Push notifications are commonly used for messages, reminders, alerts, delivery updates, and re-engagement.

They require user permission, a device token, a server that sends notifications, and platform services such as APNs for iOS or FCM for Android.

Notifications should be tied to user value. Too many low-value pushes lead users to disable permission or uninstall the app.
