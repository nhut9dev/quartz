A **course** is a structured learning unit with lessons, modules, materials, assignments, instructors, prerequisites, and learning outcomes.

Course modeling should separate authoring content from published content. Draft lessons, scheduled releases, versioned materials, and archived cohorts need different behavior.

The hard parts are content versioning, learner progress, prerequisite logic, and keeping historical records stable after course content changes.
