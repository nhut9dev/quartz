**Product-market fit** (PMF) is the point where a product satisfies strong demand in a real market. Before PMF, growth is a grind; after PMF, the market starts pulling the product out of you.

You usually feel PMF rather than measure it: usage grows by word of mouth, servers struggle to keep up, and you hire as fast as you can to meet demand.

Common signals include high retention, organic growth, and customers who would be "very disappointed" if the product disappeared. Weak retention almost always means PMF is missing.

Finding PMF is the main job of an early-stage [[lean-startup|lean startup]], usually starting from an [[../product-management/mvp|MVP]] and iterating with [[../product-management/product-discovery|product discovery]].

Until you have PMF, scaling spend on [[customer-acquisition-cost|acquisition]] or sales is premature — you'll just pour cash into a leaky product.
