**On-call** is the rotation that makes someone responsible for urgent production response.

Good on-call is not just being reachable. It needs clear alert ownership, useful runbooks, sane escalation paths, and limits that prevent burnout.

The best signal of a healthy rotation is whether alerts are actionable. If alerts regularly wake someone without requiring human judgment, improve automation or alert design. Related notes: [[../observability/alerting|Alerting]] and [[../observability/runbook|Runbook]].
