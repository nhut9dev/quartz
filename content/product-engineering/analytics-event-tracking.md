**Analytics event tracking** records product interactions so teams can understand behavior and measure outcomes.

Good events describe meaningful user actions, not low-level implementation details. They usually include an event name, user or anonymous id, timestamp, source, and carefully chosen properties.

Tracking plans help keep event names consistent and prevent dashboards from drifting as the product changes.

Analytics should respect privacy, consent, and data minimization, especially when combined with [[../security/principle-of-least-privilege|least privilege]] and retention rules.
