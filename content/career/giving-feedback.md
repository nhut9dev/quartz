**Giving feedback** is telling someone what you observed and its effect, so they can keep or change a behavior.

Use the **SBI** model: describe the Situation, the specific Behavior you saw, and the Impact it had. Facts and consequences land better than labels or judgments.

Be specific and timely. "The summary in yesterday's RFC saved me ten minutes" beats "great work," and feedback fades the longer you wait.

Aim for behavior the person can actually change, and give as much praise as you do correction.

Feedback flows best inside trust, built through [[mentoring|mentoring]] and a steady [[../engineering-management/one-on-one|one-on-one]]. The other side of the loop is [[receiving-feedback|receiving feedback]].
