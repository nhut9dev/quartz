**Seller onboarding** verifies and enables suppliers, sellers, hosts, drivers, freelancers, or service providers.

It can include identity checks, business documents, tax forms, bank accounts, product setup, training, compliance checks, and manual review.

The workflow should model pending, approved, rejected, suspended, and restricted states clearly because trust and payouts depend on them.
