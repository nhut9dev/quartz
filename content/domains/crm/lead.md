A **lead** is a potential customer who has shown interest but has not yet been qualified into a real sales opportunity.

Lead workflows include capture, enrichment, scoring, assignment, qualification, nurturing, disqualification, and conversion.

The main design challenge is preventing duplicate people or companies while preserving attribution from marketing channels and campaigns.
