---
title: Squash
description: Combining multiple Git commits into one commit.
tags:

---

**Squash** means combining multiple [[commit|commits]] into a single commit.

It is often used to turn a noisy local history into a cleaner change before [[merge|merging]].

Squashing can happen during [[interactive-rebase|interactive rebase]] or through pull request merge options.

Keep enough history to explain the work. Squashing every detail is not always better if the separate commits tell a useful story.
