The **Sprint Review** is the Scrum event for inspecting the [[increment|Increment]] and adapting the [[product-backlog|Product Backlog]] based on feedback.

The team and stakeholders discuss what changed, what was learned, what is done, and what should happen next.

It is not just a demo. A demo can be part of it, but the goal is product inspection and adaptation.

A useful Sprint Review changes understanding. It may confirm direction, reveal new risks, reorder the backlog, or adjust the [[product-goal|Product Goal]].
