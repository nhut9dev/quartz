**Transparency, inspection, and adaptation** are the three pillars that support [[empiricism|empiricism]] in Scrum.

Transparency means important information is visible and understood. Inspection means the team regularly checks progress, quality, risks, and product direction. Adaptation means the team changes course when inspection shows reality differs from the plan.

Scrum events create built-in inspection points: [[daily-scrum|Daily Scrum]] for progress, [[sprint-review|Sprint Review]] for product feedback, and [[sprint-retrospective|Sprint Retrospective]] for team improvement.

Without adaptation, inspection becomes status reporting. Without transparency, inspection is based on bad information.
