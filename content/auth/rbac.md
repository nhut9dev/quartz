---
title: RBAC
description: Role-based access control, where permissions are assigned through roles.
tags:
  - auth
---

**RBAC** stands for role-based access control.

Users receive roles such as `admin`, `editor`, or `viewer`, and roles map to permissions.

RBAC is simple to understand and works well when access patterns match stable job functions.

It becomes harder when permissions depend on resource ownership, context, or [[abac|dynamic attributes]].
