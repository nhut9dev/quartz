---
title: ABAC
description: Attribute-based access control, where access decisions use user, resource, and context attributes.
tags:
  - auth
---

**ABAC** stands for attribute-based access control.

It makes authorization decisions from attributes such as user department, resource owner, region, time, or risk level.

ABAC can express more flexible policies than [[rbac|role-based access control]], but it requires clear policy design and good test coverage.
