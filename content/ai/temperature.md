**Temperature** affects how deterministic or varied [[llm|model]] output feels.

Lower temperature usually makes responses more focused and repeatable.

Higher temperature can increase variety, but may also increase inconsistency.

Use lower settings for extraction, classification, and code. Use higher settings when exploring alternatives.
