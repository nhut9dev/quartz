---
title: Skip link
description: A keyboard link that jumps past repeated navigation to the main content.
tags:
  - accessibility
---

A **skip link** lets [[keyboard-navigation|keyboard users]] jump directly to the main content.

It is usually the first focusable [[anchor-and-link|link]] on the page and becomes visible when focused.

```html
<a href="#main">Skip to main content</a>
<main id="main">...</main>
```

Skip links are especially important on pages with repeated headers, menus, or sidebars.
