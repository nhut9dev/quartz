---
title: SLO and SLI
description: Reliability targets and the measurements used to evaluate them.
tags:
  - observability
---

An **SLI** is a service-level indicator, such as request success rate or latency percentile, derived from [[metrics|metrics]].

An **SLO** is a service-level objective, such as 99.9% successful requests over 30 days.

SLOs help teams define reliability in user-facing terms and decide when to prioritize stability over feature work.
