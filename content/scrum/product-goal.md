The **Product Goal** is a long-term objective for the product. It gives direction to the [[product-backlog|Product Backlog]].

The [[product-owner|Product Owner]] is accountable for communicating the Product Goal and ordering backlog work toward it.

A useful Product Goal is specific enough to guide tradeoffs. It helps the team decide what matters now and what can wait.

Without a Product Goal, the backlog can become a list of disconnected requests.
