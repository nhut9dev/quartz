**Personal brand** is the reputation you build through visible, consistent work, what people expect when they hear your name.

You can't fake it. A brand is the accumulated trail of what you ship, how you treat people, and the problems you reliably solve.

Make good work visible. Write up what you learn, share useful tools, and speak up where it helps, so your impact isn't invisible.

Consistency matters more than any single splash. Showing up dependably over years beats one viral moment.

It grows naturally from [[continuous-learning|continuous learning]], clear [[technical-writing|technical writing]], and the way you handle [[code-review-etiquette|code review]] and [[mentoring|mentoring]].
