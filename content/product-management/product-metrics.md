**Product metrics** are the quantitative signals teams use to measure whether a product is working. Choosing the right ones is half the battle; the wrong metric quietly steers a team in the wrong direction.

*Leading* metrics predict future outcomes and can be influenced now, like activation rate or weekly active usage. *Lagging* metrics confirm results after the fact, like revenue or churn. You steer with leading and verify with lagging.

*Vanity* metrics look impressive but don't drive decisions, like total signups or page views. *Actionable* metrics tie to a decision and a cause you can change, like conversion rate by source.

A useful metric is comparable, ratio-based, and tied to user behavior. If a number going up wouldn't change what you do next, it isn't earning its place on the dashboard.

Product metrics underpin the [[north-star-metric|north-star metric]], the [[aarrr-metrics|AARRR funnel]], and every [[ab-testing|A/B test]]. They depend on accurate [[../product-engineering/analytics-event-tracking|analytics event tracking]].
