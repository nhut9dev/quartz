**Layout and composition** is the arrangement of elements so a design feels organized and usable.

### How it works

Layout defines the structure: grid, columns, regions, navigation, content area, and controls. Composition decides how those pieces balance, align, and lead the eye through the page.

Good composition uses grouping, proximity, repetition, alignment, and contrast. In web work, it must also survive responsive changes through [[../css/grid|grid]], [[../css/flexbox|flexbox]], and [[../css/responsive-design|responsive design]].

### When to use

Use layout thinking before styling details. A screen with clear structure can tolerate plain visual styling; a screen with weak structure will remain confusing even with polished colors and typography.

### Trade-offs

Dense layouts support expert workflows and comparison. Spacious layouts support comprehension and focus. The right choice depends on frequency of use, task complexity, and screen size.

### Pitfalls

Avoid decorating around a weak layout. Misaligned edges, inconsistent groups, and unclear reading order usually hurt usability more than missing visual flourish.
