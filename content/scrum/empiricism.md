---
title: Empiricism
description: Making decisions from observed evidence instead of assumptions.
tags:
  - scrum
  - agile
---

**Empiricism** means making decisions from what is observed and learned, not only from plans or assumptions.

In Scrum, empiricism shows up through short [[sprint|Sprints]], visible work, usable [[increment|Increments]], and regular inspection points.

```txt
build -> inspect -> learn -> adapt
```

Empiricism matters because complex product work cannot be fully predicted upfront. The team learns by delivering something real and seeing what happens.
