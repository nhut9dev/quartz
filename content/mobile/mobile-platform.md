A **mobile platform** is the operating system, APIs, tools, device behaviors, and design conventions that mobile apps run on.

The two main consumer platforms are [[ios|iOS]] and [[android|Android]]. They differ in permissions, navigation patterns, build tooling, app distribution, background execution, and UI conventions.

Cross-platform tools like [[react-native-introduction|React Native]] reduce duplication, but they do not erase platform differences.

Good mobile apps respect the platform they are running on, especially around gestures, safe areas, permissions, notifications, and back behavior.
