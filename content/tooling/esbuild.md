**esbuild** is a very fast [[bundler]] and transformer.

It is often used inside other tools for TypeScript [[transpiler|transpilation]], [[minification]], dependency prebundling, and development pipelines.

Its speed makes it valuable for feedback loops, though some advanced plugin ecosystems are richer in older bundlers.
