---
title: Layout shift
description: A visible movement of page content after it has already rendered.
tags:
  - css
  - web
---

A **layout shift** happens when visible content moves unexpectedly after the page has already rendered.

Common causes include images without dimensions, late-loading fonts, injected banners, ads, skeletons with different sizes, and expanding components.

Layout shift hurts perceived quality because users may lose their place or click the wrong target.

Reserve stable space for media and dynamic UI. Use dimensions, aspect ratios, predictable placeholders, and consistent loading states.
