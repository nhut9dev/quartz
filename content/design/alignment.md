**Alignment** is placing elements on shared edges or axes so a layout feels ordered.

Aligned elements are easier to scan because the eye can follow predictable lines. Forms, tables, cards, toolbars, and navigation all depend on clear alignment.

Breaking alignment can create emphasis, but it should be intentional. Accidental near-alignment often feels sloppy because elements are close enough to look related but not close enough to feel precise.

Alignment works with [[spacing|spacing]] and [[layout-and-composition|layout and composition]].
