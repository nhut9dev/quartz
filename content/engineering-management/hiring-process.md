A **hiring process** is the pipeline that moves a candidate from sourcing to offer: outreach, screening, an interview loop, debrief, and decision. A clear process keeps it fair and fast.

Sourcing and screening filter for basic fit before investing the team's time. A structured loop then gathers deeper signal across several focused sessions, each owned by a different interviewer.

**Scorecards** make the decision honest. Each interviewer writes their assessment against agreed criteria *before* the debrief, so the room is not swayed by the loudest voice.

Structure reduces bias and improves signal. Asking every candidate comparable questions makes their answers actually comparable.

The loop usually includes a [[technical-interview|technical interview]] plus behavioral rounds. Hiring against a defined [[career-ladder|career ladder]] level keeps expectations consistent across candidates.
