**Team health** is how well a team can keep delivering without burning out: morale, sustainable pace, trust, and resilience. It is a leading indicator of long-term output.

A healthy team works at a pace it could hold for years. Constant crunch may ship a quarter faster but trades away the next several, as quality and people both degrade.

Watch for burnout signals: rising cynicism, dropping engagement, slipping quality, quiet quitting, and people skipping breaks or working late by default.

Health is protected, not assumed. Realistic scope, recovery time after crunch, fair on-call load, and acting on retro feedback all keep it intact.

Managers read the signals through [[one-on-one|one-on-ones]] and [[psychological-safety|psychological safety]]. Sustainable load also depends on a humane [[../engineering-process/on-call|On-call]] rotation and healthy [[../scrum/sprint-retrospective|retrospectives]].
