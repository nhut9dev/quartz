An **app store** is a platform distribution channel for mobile apps. The main public stores are Apple's App Store and Google Play.

Stores handle listing pages, reviews, downloads, updates, ratings, payments, and policy enforcement.

Shipping through an app store adds requirements that web deployments do not have: signing, version codes, screenshots, review guidelines, privacy labels, and staged releases.

Teams often use testing channels like TestFlight, internal testing, or closed tracks before releasing to everyone.
