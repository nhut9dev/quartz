A **Sprint** is a fixed-length Scrum event where the team works toward a [[sprint-goal|Sprint Goal]] and creates a usable [[increment|Increment]].

Sprints are usually one month or less. A shorter Sprint gives faster feedback; a longer Sprint gives more room for work but delays learning.

The Sprint contains all other Scrum events: [[sprint-planning|Sprint Planning]], [[daily-scrum|Daily Scrum]], [[sprint-review|Sprint Review]], and [[sprint-retrospective|Sprint Retrospective]].

During a Sprint, scope may be clarified and renegotiated, but changes should not endanger the Sprint Goal.
