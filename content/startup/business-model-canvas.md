The **business model canvas** is a one-page template that describes how a business creates, delivers, and captures value. It replaces a long business plan with a single visual you can sketch and revise quickly.

It has nine blocks: customer segments, value propositions, channels, customer relationships, revenue streams, key resources, key activities, key partnerships, and cost structure.

The right side describes value and demand (who you serve and how you earn), while the left side describes the machinery (what it takes to deliver). [[recurring-revenue|Revenue streams]] minus cost structure is where profit lives.

It pairs naturally with [[lean-startup|lean startup]]: each block is an assumption you can test, and a [[go-to-market|go-to-market]] plan flows out of channels and segments.

Treat the canvas as a hypothesis, not a finished design — you'll redraw it many times before reaching [[product-market-fit|product-market fit]].
