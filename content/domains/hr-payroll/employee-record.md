title: Employee record

---

An **employee record** stores employment information such as identity, job, department, manager, location, start date, status, compensation, documents, and lifecycle events.

Many fields are date-effective. A promotion, manager change, salary change, or termination should preserve historical meaning for payroll and reporting.

Access control is central because employee records contain sensitive personal and compensation data.
