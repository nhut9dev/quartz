A **career ladder** is a framework that describes what is expected at each engineering level, from junior through senior, staff, and beyond. It makes growth legible.

Each level is usually defined by scope of impact, autonomy, and influence rather than years served. A senior engineer owns a system; a staff engineer shapes work across teams.

A good ladder names observable behaviors, not vibes. "Drives ambiguous projects to done" is actionable; "is senior" is not.

The ladder is the shared yardstick for [[performance-review|performance reviews]], [[../career/giving-feedback|Giving feedback]], and promotion decisions. It keeps those conversations grounded in the same definitions.

It typically branches into the [[ic-vs-management-track|IC and management tracks]] at the senior level, with a [[tech-lead|tech lead]] role often bridging the two.
