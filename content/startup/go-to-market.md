**Go-to-market** (GTM) is the plan for how a company reaches, wins, and keeps customers. It answers who you sell to, through which channels, with what message, and at what price.

GTM motions vary by deal size. Self-serve and product-led growth let users sign up alone; inside sales handles mid-market deals over calls; field sales pursues large enterprise contracts in person.

Channels are how you reach buyers: content and SEO, paid ads, partnerships, communities, or outbound. The right channel is the one that reaches your segment cheaply and repeatably.

GTM is tightly linked to [[pricing-strategy|pricing]] and to [[customer-acquisition-cost|acquisition cost]] — a channel only works if a customer costs less to win than they're worth.

Early on, GTM is really part of [[product-market-fit|finding product-market fit]]: you experiment until you find a repeatable way to turn strangers into paying customers.
