An **RFC** (request for comments) is a written proposal that describes a design or change and invites feedback before any code is written.

It typically states the problem, the proposed approach, alternatives considered, and the tradeoffs, so reviewers can critique the direction early.

The goal is to surface disagreement and risk while changes are still cheap. By the time discussion settles, the team shares context and the build becomes mechanical.

An RFC captures a proposal under debate; once a specific choice is locked in, record it as an [[architecture-decision-record|ADR]].

Good RFCs depend on [[../career/technical-writing|technical writing]] and [[../career/async-communication|async communication]], and feed a healthy [[documentation-culture|documentation culture]].
