An **LLM** is a large language model trained to predict and generate language-like sequences.

LLMs can answer questions, summarize, classify, extract data, write code, [[tool-use|call tools]], and produce structured output.

They are probabilistic systems. Good applications design around uncertainty, validation, grounding, and user feedback.
