**Async communication** is collaborating through written, time-shifted messages instead of requiring everyone present at once.

It trades immediacy for reach: people respond on their own schedule, across time zones, without interrupting their focus.

The cost is that writing must stand alone. Give full context, state the decision you need, and set a clear deadline so the reader can act without a follow-up.

Default to async for anything that can wait, and reserve synchronous time for ambiguity, conflict, or fast iteration.

It pairs with strong [[technical-writing|technical writing]] and protects [[deep-work|deep work]]. Decisions worth recording belong in an [[../engineering-process/rfc|RFC]] or doc, not a chat thread.
