**RabbitMQ** is a message broker often used as a [[message-queue|message queue]] for task queues and service communication.

It supports exchanges, queues, routing keys, acknowledgements, retries, and dead-letter queues.

It is a good fit for work queues and routing patterns in [[event-driven|event-driven]] systems where broker-managed delivery semantics matter.
