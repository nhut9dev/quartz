title: Onboarding
description: The first-run experience that helps users reach meaningful value.
tags:

---

**Onboarding** helps users understand enough to get value without turning the product into a tutorial.

Good onboarding is contextual. It asks for only necessary setup, uses real user goals, and gets people to a successful first action quickly.

Measure onboarding by activation and retention, not by whether users finished every step. Remove steps that do not help users succeed.
