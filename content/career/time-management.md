**Time management** is choosing what to work on and protecting time for it, more than squeezing more tasks into a day.

Prioritize by impact, not urgency. A loud, small task can crowd out the quiet work that actually moves the goal.

Saying no is the core skill. Every yes is a commitment of hours you can't spend elsewhere, so guard them deliberately.

Batch shallow work and reserve uninterrupted blocks for the hard things. Context switching is a hidden tax on everything.

It enables [[deep-work|deep work]] and depends on clear [[async-communication|async communication]] so you aren't pulled into every thread.
