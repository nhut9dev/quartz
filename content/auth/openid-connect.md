  - auth
---

**OpenID Connect** adds authentication and identity information on top of OAuth 2.0.

[[oauth|OAuth]] answers authorization questions: what can this client access?

OpenID Connect answers login identity questions: who is this user?

It commonly returns an ID token and exposes user profile information through standard claims.
