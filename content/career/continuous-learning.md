**Continuous learning** is the steady habit of staying current and getting better, long after formal schooling ends.

In a fast-moving field, skills decay. Setting aside regular time to learn keeps you from quietly falling behind.

Favor deliberate practice over passive consumption. Pick something just past your reach, work at it, and seek feedback on the result.

Learn in public when you can. Writing notes, shipping small projects, or explaining a concept cements it far better than reading alone.

It feeds on [[receiving-feedback|receiving feedback]] and steady [[deep-work|deep work]], and the visible output builds your [[personal-brand|personal brand]].
