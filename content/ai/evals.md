**Evals** measure whether an AI system behaves well enough for a task.

They can check accuracy, formatting, safety, latency, cost, retrieval quality, and user-facing outcomes.

Good evals use realistic examples and clear scoring criteria, not only happy-path prompts.

They are important because changes to prompts, models, [[rag|RAG]], and tools can improve one case while breaking another.
