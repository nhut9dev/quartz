**Runway** is how many months a startup can keep operating before it runs out of cash. It is the single number that defines how much time you have to make progress.

You calculate it by dividing cash in the bank by **burn rate** — the net cash you spend each month. With $600k in the bank and $50k monthly burn, you have twelve months of runway.

Net burn counts revenue too: if you spend $50k but earn $20k, your burn is $30k and your runway is longer. Growing revenue extends runway just like cutting costs does.

Runway shapes every decision: when to raise the next round of [[fundraising|fundraising]], how aggressively to spend on [[customer-acquisition-cost|acquisition]], and whether you can afford to keep searching for [[product-market-fit|product-market fit]].

A common rule is to raise with 12–18 months of runway, because fundraising itself takes months and you never want to negotiate from a position of desperation.
