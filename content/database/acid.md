---
title: ACID
description: "Four database transaction properties: atomicity, consistency, isolation, and durability."
tags:
  - database
---

**ACID** describes important transaction guarantees in many relational databases.

Atomicity means all operations in a transaction succeed or fail together. Consistency means constraints remain valid. Isolation controls how transactions interact. Durability means committed data survives failures.

ACID is most important when correctness matters more than accepting temporary inconsistency.
