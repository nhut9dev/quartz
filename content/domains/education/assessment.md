An **assessment** measures learner understanding through quizzes, exams, assignments, rubrics, projects, or manual grading.

Assessment systems must define attempts, time limits, scoring rules, retakes, feedback visibility, question randomization, and grading ownership.

High-stakes assessments need stronger controls for auditability, integrity, late submissions, accessibility accommodations, and dispute handling.
