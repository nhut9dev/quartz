**Engineering onboarding** is the process of ramping a new engineer to productive, confident contribution as quickly as possible.

The fastest onboarding ships a small real change in the first days, then expands scope, rather than front-loading weeks of passive reading. Shipping early builds the mental model that documentation alone cannot.

Good onboarding leans on existing artifacts instead of one person's memory: setup guides, [[architecture-decision-record|ADRs]], and [[../observability/runbook|runbooks]] let newcomers self-serve answers.

A clear first task, a named buddy, and a checklist reduce the silent friction where new hires get stuck without knowing whom to ask.

Strong onboarding is a direct payoff of [[documentation-culture|documentation culture]], and the [[code-review-process|code review process]] keeps teaching context long after week one.
