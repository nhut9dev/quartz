A **purchase order** is a formal buying document sent to a supplier.

It usually contains supplier information, item lines, quantities, expected prices, delivery terms, tax information, shipping destination, and approval status. A purchase order is a commitment to buy, not proof that goods have already been received.

Important states include draft, pending approval, approved, sent, partially received, received, closed, and cancelled. These states affect [[inventory|Inventory]], supplier communication, and financial obligations.
