**Healthcare** domain knowledge covers patient care workflows, clinical records, scheduling, prescriptions, billing, privacy, and interoperability.

Healthcare systems have high sensitivity because data is personal, workflows affect care quality, and access rules can be complex. Notes in this folder focus on the concepts an engineer needs before designing data models or APIs.
