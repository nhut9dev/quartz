title: Delegation

**Delegation** is handing someone ownership of an outcome, not just a list of tasks. Done well, it grows people and frees the manager to work on higher-leverage problems.

The key is delegating the *what* and the *why* while leaving the *how* to the person. Prescribing every step is just assigning work; it teaches little and signals distrust.

Effective delegation matches the size of the stretch to the person's current level. Too small bores them; too large sets them up to fail and damages confidence.

Ownership still needs support: agree on the goal, check in on a cadence, and stay available without hovering. This is delegation, not abdication.

Delegation is how managers scale and a core form of [[../career/mentoring|Mentoring]]. It pairs with clear [[career-ladder|career ladder]] expectations and steady [[../career/giving-feedback|Giving feedback]].
