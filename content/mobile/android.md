**Android** is Google's mobile operating system used across phones, tablets, TVs, and many device vendors.

Android apps are built with Android tooling, packaged as APK or AAB files, and commonly distributed through Google Play or internal channels.

React Native apps targeting Android still need native Android configuration for permissions, Gradle, manifests, icons, build variants, and native dependencies.

Android-specific behavior often appears around the back button, permissions, notification channels, device fragmentation, and emulator setup.
