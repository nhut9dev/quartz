**Dispute resolution** handles conflicts between marketplace participants, such as item not received, damaged goods, poor service, cancellation, chargeback, or payout disagreement.

The workflow usually needs evidence collection, communication, support review, policy application, decision, refund or payout action, and audit history.

Good systems separate the dispute record from the financial action so support decisions can be reviewed before money moves.
