A **postmortem** is a written review of an incident after the service has recovered.

It should document impact, timeline, contributing factors, detection gaps, response quality, and concrete follow-up actions.

Good postmortems focus on system conditions and decision points rather than blame. The outcome should improve alerts, tests, rollback, automation, documentation, or architecture.

Postmortems turn [[incident-response|incident response]] into reliability learning.
