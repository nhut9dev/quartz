**Last-mile delivery** is the final leg where goods move from a local hub or courier to the recipient.

It involves delivery windows, driver assignment, route optimization, proof of delivery, failed attempts, customer notifications, and returns to depot.

The system must handle real-world uncertainty: traffic, wrong addresses, unavailable recipients, offline mobile devices, and late carrier events.
