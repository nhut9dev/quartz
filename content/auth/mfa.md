---
title: MFA
description: Multi-factor authentication using more than one proof of identity.
tags:
  - auth

---

**MFA** requires more than one authentication factor.

Factors include something you know, something you have, and something you are.

MFA reduces account takeover risk when passwords are guessed, reused, or leaked, even when [[password-hashing|password storage]] is done correctly.

Phishing-resistant factors such as passkeys or hardware security keys are stronger than one-time codes.
