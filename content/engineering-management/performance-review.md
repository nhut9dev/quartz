A **performance review** evaluates an engineer's impact over a period against the expectations of their level. Its goal is an accurate, useful picture, not a surprise.

Nothing in a review should be new. If feedback only appears here, the manager has failed at [[../career/giving-feedback|Giving feedback]] during the cycle, when it could still have helped.

Common biases distort reviews. **Recency bias** overweights the last few weeks; **halo bias** lets one strong trait inflate everything else. Notes gathered throughout the period counter both.

Reviews work best anchored to concrete examples and tied to a shared [[career-ladder|career ladder]]. Vague praise or criticism gives the person nothing to act on.

A fair, consistent review process protects [[psychological-safety|psychological safety]] and informs decisions about [[managing-underperformance|underperformance]] and promotion.
