The **Product Owner** is accountable for maximizing the value of the product created by the [[scrum-team|Scrum Team]].

The Product Owner manages the [[product-backlog|Product Backlog]], orders work by value and learning, communicates the [[product-goal|Product Goal]], and makes tradeoffs clear.

This does not mean the Product Owner writes every backlog item alone. They are accountable for the backlog being useful, transparent, and ordered.

A weak Product Owner role often shows up as unclear priorities, too many urgent changes, or backlog items that describe output without product intent.
