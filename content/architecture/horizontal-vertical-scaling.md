---
title: Horizontal and vertical scaling
description: Two ways to increase system capacity.
tags:
  - architecture
---

**Vertical scaling** gives one machine more CPU, memory, or disk.

**Horizontal scaling** adds more machines or instances.

Vertical scaling is often simpler but has hardware limits. Horizontal scaling improves capacity and availability, but requires stateless services, [[load-balancing|load balancing]], and shared data strategy.
