A **sales pipeline** is the ordered set of stages an opportunity moves through from qualification to closed won or closed lost.

Pipeline stages should represent real buyer or seller progress, not just a UI board column.

Good CRM systems make stage changes observable because forecasting, coaching, automation, and revenue reporting depend on accurate pipeline movement.
