A **claim** is a request for the insurer to pay money or provide a service under a policy.

Claim workflows usually include intake, document collection, eligibility checks, coverage review, adjustment, approval, payment, denial, appeal, and closure.

The key engineering concern is traceability. Every decision should connect to policy terms, submitted evidence, adjuster actions, rules, and timestamps.
