title: Product roadmap
description: Communicating product direction through outcomes rather than a fixed feature list.

A **product roadmap** communicates the direction of a product over time and the reasoning behind it. It's a tool for alignment, not a binding delivery contract.

A *feature-list* roadmap commits to specific features on specific dates. It feels concrete but tends to break under change and shifts focus from outcomes to output.

An *outcome-based* roadmap organizes work around problems to solve and goals to hit, like "reduce onboarding drop-off," leaving solutions flexible. It survives learning and keeps teams focused on value.

Good roadmaps express confidence honestly, often as now / next / later horizons rather than precise dates. Near-term items are detailed; distant ones stay deliberately vague.

The roadmap draws from [[product-discovery|discovery]], a [[prioritization-framework|prioritization framework]], and the [[north-star-metric|north-star metric]] to keep direction tied to real value.
