**Technical debt** is the accumulated cost of shortcuts, aging design, and decay that make future changes slower and riskier.

Deliberate debt is a conscious tradeoff, taking a shortcut to ship now with a plan to repay later. Accidental debt creeps in through eroding design and changing requirements, often unnoticed until it bites.

Like financial debt, it carries interest: every delayed payment makes the next change a little harder. A small amount is healthy leverage; left unmanaged, it compounds into paralysis.

Managing paydown means making debt visible and budgeting for it, rather than hoping for a mythical free week to clean up.

Debt surfaces in slowing [[estimation|estimates]] and dropping [[../scrum/velocity|velocity]], and is often discussed in the [[../scrum/sprint-retrospective|Sprint Retrospective]].
