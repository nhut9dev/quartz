**Code review etiquette** is the set of habits that make your reviews kind, clear, and useful to the author.

Review the code, not the person. Comment on the change, ask questions instead of issuing verdicts, and assume the author had reasons you can't see.

Separate must-fix from nice-to-have. Label preferences as optional so the author knows what blocks merge and what is just your taste.

Be timely. A fast, rough review unblocks a teammate more than a perfect review that arrives a day late.

This is the individual half of the team-level [[../engineering-process/code-review-process|code review process]]. Done well, it doubles as [[mentoring|mentoring]] and a channel for [[giving-feedback|giving feedback]].
