The **Sprint Retrospective** is the Scrum event for inspecting how the team worked and choosing improvements.

The team can inspect communication, tools, quality, process, collaboration, technical practices, and sources of friction.

The point is not to collect complaints. The point is to pick realistic changes that make the next [[sprint|Sprint]] better.

Good retrospectives produce action, ownership, and follow-through. Without that, they become repetitive meetings with the same problems every time.
