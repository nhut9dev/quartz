---
title: Context window
description: The maximum amount of input and output a model can consider in one request.
tags:
  - ai

---

A **context window** is the amount of information a model can consider in a single request.

It includes instructions, conversation history, retrieved documents, [[tool-use|tool results]], and generated output.

Large context helps, but irrelevant context can distract the [[llm|model]] and increase cost.
