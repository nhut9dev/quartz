**Acceptance criteria** are conditions that clarify when a backlog item behaves as expected.

```txt
- Invite email is sent to a valid address.
- Existing members cannot be invited twice.
- Invalid emails show a clear error message.
```

They help the team and stakeholders agree on scope, behavior, and edge cases before implementation.

Acceptance criteria are not the same as the [[definition-of-done|Definition of Done]]. Acceptance criteria are specific to one item; Definition of Done is a shared quality standard for all increments.
