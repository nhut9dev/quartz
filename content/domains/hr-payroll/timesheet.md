A **timesheet** records work time for payroll, client billing, compliance, or project accounting.

Timesheet workflows include entry, reminders, submission, approval, correction, locking, export, and audit.

Important details include overtime rules, time zones, rounding, break rules, project allocation, and whether submitted periods can be reopened.
