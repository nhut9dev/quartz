A **commission** is the fee a marketplace takes from a transaction, booking, payout, or service.

Commission rules can depend on category, seller tier, region, promotion, payment method, refund state, taxes, and negotiated contracts.

The system should store enough detail to explain how each fee was calculated later, especially when sellers dispute payouts.
