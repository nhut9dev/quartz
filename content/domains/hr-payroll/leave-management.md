**Leave management** handles time-off requests, approvals, balances, holidays, policies, accruals, carryover, and payroll impact.

Leave rules vary by country, employment type, company policy, tenure, and leave category.

The system must handle overlapping requests, partial days, manager delegation, policy changes, and corrections after payroll has already run.
