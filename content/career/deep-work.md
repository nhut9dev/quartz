**Deep work** is sustained, undistracted focus on cognitively demanding tasks that create disproportionate value.

Hard problems need continuous attention. Each interruption costs not just the minutes lost but the time to rebuild your mental state.

Protect it with structure: scheduled blocks, silenced notifications, and a clear single goal for the session.

It is a skill you train, not a mood you wait for. Start with short focused stretches and extend them as your stamina grows.

It is the payoff of good [[time-management|time management]] and a culture of [[async-communication|async communication]] that doesn't demand instant replies.
