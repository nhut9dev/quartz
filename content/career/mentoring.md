**Mentoring** is helping another person grow by sharing context, asking guiding questions, and giving honest feedback over time.

Teach the reasoning, not just the answer. When you explain why a tradeoff matters, the mentee can apply it to problems you'll never see.

Match support to the moment: more direction when someone is new, more space as they gain confidence. The goal is independence, not dependence.

Mentoring compounds your impact and sharpens your own thinking, since explaining a thing forces you to understand it.

It often happens through [[code-review-etiquette|code review]], a recurring [[../engineering-management/one-on-one|one-on-one]], and steady [[giving-feedback|feedback]].
