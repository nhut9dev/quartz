A **story point** is a relative estimate of the size of a backlog item.

Story points usually combine effort, uncertainty, complexity, and risk. They are not hours.

```txt
1 point  small and clear
3 points moderate
8 points large or uncertain
```

Story points can help with forecasting through [[velocity|velocity]], but they become harmful when used as individual performance targets.
