**Domain knowledge** is the business context, vocabulary, workflows, constraints, and failure modes that shape software systems.

These notes are separate from general technical topics like [[../database/|Database]], [[../api-design/|API design]], and [[../system-design/|System design]]. The goal is to explain what the business problem means before linking it to implementation choices.
