**Lean startup** is a method for building a company through fast, cheap cycles of experimentation rather than long upfront planning. The goal is to find a working business model before running out of [[runway|runway]].

Its core engine is the **build-measure-learn** loop: build the smallest thing that tests an idea, measure how real users respond, and learn whether to keep going or change course.

The output of each loop is **validated learning** — evidence about customers, not just opinions or vanity metrics. A cheap experiment that kills a bad idea early is a success.

You usually start with an [[../product-management/mvp|MVP]]: the minimum product that lets you run a real test with real users.

When learning shows the current direction won't work, you **pivot** — change strategy while keeping what you've learned. Lean startup is how teams hunt for [[product-market-fit|product-market fit]] without betting everything on a single guess.
