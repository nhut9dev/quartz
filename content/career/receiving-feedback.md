**Receiving feedback** is taking in others' observations without defensiveness and using what's useful to improve.

Your first instinct is often to explain or defend. Pause, listen fully, and ask clarifying questions before you respond.

Separate the signal from the delivery. Even clumsy feedback can hold a true point, and you don't have to accept all of it to learn from part of it.

Close the loop: thank the person, decide what you'll change, and follow up. Acting on feedback is what makes people keep offering it.

This is the receiving end of [[giving-feedback|giving feedback]], and a core habit in [[continuous-learning|continuous learning]]. A trusting [[../engineering-management/one-on-one|one-on-one]] makes hard feedback easier to hear.
