**Developers** are the [[scrum-team|Scrum Team]] members accountable for creating a usable [[increment|Increment]] each [[sprint|Sprint]].

In Scrum, "Developers" means the people who do the work of delivering the product, not only software engineers. Depending on the product, this can include design, testing, analysis, engineering, documentation, and other skills.

Developers plan the Sprint work, adapt the plan daily, maintain quality, and hold each other accountable to the [[definition-of-done|Definition of Done]].

They own the "how" of turning backlog items into a done increment.
