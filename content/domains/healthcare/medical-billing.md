**Medical billing** turns healthcare services into claims, invoices, reimbursements, and payment records.

The workflow can involve patient eligibility, service coding, diagnosis coding, claim submission, payer adjudication, denial handling, patient responsibility, and payment posting. A clinical event and a billable event are related but not identical.

Billing systems need careful status modeling because denied, pending, partially paid, adjusted, and written-off amounts mean different things. This connects to [[../finance/business/invoice|Invoice]], [[../finance/business/reconciliation|Reconciliation]], and audit trails.
