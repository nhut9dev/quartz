A **Route Handler** defines request handlers such as `GET`, `POST`, `PUT`, or `DELETE` in the [[app-router|App Router]].

They are commonly used for APIs, webhooks, auth callbacks, and server-only integrations.

Route Handlers are lower-level than pages. They return [[http|HTTP]] responses directly.
