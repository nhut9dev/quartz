**Image optimization** reduces image bytes and layout cost.

Use the right format, dimensions, compression level, [[lazy-loading|lazy loading]], and responsive sources.

Images should reserve space with width, height, or aspect ratio to avoid [[../css/layout-shift|layout shift]].

Optimization is most valuable for hero images, product galleries, avatars, thumbnails, and content feeds.
