An **opportunity** is a qualified sales deal being worked by a sales team.

It usually has an account, contacts, owner, stage, expected value, probability, close date, activities, competitors, and next steps.

Opportunity data drives forecasting, so stage definitions and transition rules matter more than they first appear.
