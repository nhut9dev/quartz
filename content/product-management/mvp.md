A **minimum viable product** (MVP) is the smallest version of a product that lets you test a core hypothesis with real users and real learning. Its purpose is validated learning, not a stripped-down launch.

The key word is *viable*: an MVP must actually deliver enough value for users to engage, so the signal you collect is genuine. A broken or trivial slice teaches you nothing.

MVPs come in many shapes, from a landing page to a concierge "do it manually" service to a single working feature. Choose the cheapest form that still answers your riskiest question.

Treat the MVP as an experiment with a clear hypothesis and success metric, closely related to [[ab-testing|A/B testing]] and [[product-metrics|product metrics]]. If the signal is weak, pivot before scaling.

The MVP is a central tool of [[product-discovery|product discovery]] and a common path toward [[../startup/product-market-fit|product-market fit]]. Ship it behind a [[../deployment/feature-flag|feature flag]] to control exposure.
