**Prompt engineering** is the practice of shaping instructions and [[context-window|context]] for [[llm|model]] behavior.

Good prompts clarify task, constraints, output format, examples, and what information should be trusted.

Prompting is part of the system design. It works best with validation, [[rag|retrieval]], [[tool-use|tools]], tests, and clear product behavior.
