**Title tag** is the HTML title used by browsers, search results, and many link previews.

A good title names the page clearly, matches search intent, and distinguishes the page from nearby pages. It should usually include the main topic and, when useful, the brand or product name.

Titles are part of [[on-page-seo|on-page SEO]] and live alongside [[meta-description|meta descriptions]] and other [[../web/meta-tags|meta tags]].

Avoid duplicate titles, keyword lists, vague labels like "Home", and titles that promise something the page does not actually deliver.
