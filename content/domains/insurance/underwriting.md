**Underwriting** evaluates risk before issuing, renewing, or pricing an insurance policy.

It can use application data, third-party data, rules, human review, risk scores, and historical claims information.

Underwriting systems need explainability, versioned rules, exception handling, and strong audit trails because pricing and eligibility decisions can be disputed.
