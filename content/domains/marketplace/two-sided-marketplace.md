A **two-sided marketplace** connects two participant groups that depend on each other, such as buyers and sellers or riders and drivers.

The product must solve cold start, liquidity, trust, pricing, quality control, and matching.

Engineering systems often need search, ranking, availability, payments, reviews, moderation, fraud detection, and operational tooling for support teams.
