**Procurement** is the workflow for obtaining goods or services from suppliers.

The process often starts with a request, moves through approval, becomes a [[purchase-order|purchase order]], then continues into receiving, invoicing, and payment. Each stage has different owners and controls.

Procurement systems need clear status transitions, supplier records, budget checks, approval rules, and links to inventory and accounting. The technical shape often involves workflow state, permissions, notifications, and audit trails.
