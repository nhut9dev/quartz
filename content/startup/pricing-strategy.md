**Pricing strategy** is how a company sets prices to capture a fair share of the value it creates. It's one of the highest-leverage decisions a startup makes, yet often the least examined.

**Cost-plus pricing** marks up what the product costs to make. **Value-based pricing** sets price by what the outcome is worth to the customer, which usually captures far more — and is the better default for software.

**Packaging** splits the product into tiers (good/better/best) so different segments self-select. Tiers anchor perceived value and create a clear path to upgrade as a customer grows.

Price connects directly to [[unit-economics|unit economics]] and [[recurring-revenue|recurring revenue]]: a small price increase often flows straight to contribution margin since costs barely change.

Pricing also shapes your [[go-to-market|go-to-market]] motion — low prices favor self-serve, while high prices justify a sales team to close each deal.
