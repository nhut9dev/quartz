**Technical writing** is producing clear, structured documents that help readers understand a problem and decide what to do, fast.

Lead with the conclusion, then support it. Engineers skim, so put the answer first and the reasoning after.

Write for one reader at a time. Name your audience, cut jargon they won't know, and define terms before you lean on them.

Structure carries meaning: short paragraphs, descriptive headings, and lists for parallel items. The shape of the page should match the shape of the idea.

This skill compounds across an [[../engineering-process/rfc|RFC]], a pull request, and a [[../engineering-process/documentation-culture|documentation culture]]. Good writing also powers [[async-communication|async communication]].
