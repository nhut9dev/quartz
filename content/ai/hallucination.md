A **hallucination** is an unsupported or false [[llm|model]] output.

It can happen when the model lacks information, overgeneralizes, misreads context, or is pushed to answer anyway.

Mitigations include [[rag|retrieval]], citations, [[tool-use|tool use]], refusing unknowns, constrained outputs, validation, and human review for high-risk tasks.
