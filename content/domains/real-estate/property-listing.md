A **property listing** represents a property available for sale, rent, lease, or booking.

Listings include address, location, price, availability, photos, amenities, rules, ownership, agent information, and publication status.

The main challenges are search, duplicate listings, stale availability, photo moderation, map filtering, SEO, and permissions for unpublished or private properties.
